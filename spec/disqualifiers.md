# Disqualifiers

> **Replace everything below.** Sample content from a fictional vendor.

Disqualifiers are more valuable than qualifiers and almost nobody writes them
down. A qualifier tells an agent where to look. A disqualifier stops it wasting
your quarter.

Each one states the signal, what it predicts, and how confident you are.

---

## Hard — do not pursue

- **No budget owner identified after two calls**
  `set: 2026-02-01` · `evidence: 9 deals, 0 closed` · `confidence: strong`
- **Evaluating as a compliance box-tick with no operational sponsor**
  `set: 2026-01-20` · `evidence: 6 deals, 1 closed, that one churned` · `confidence: moderate`

## Soft — proceed with eyes open

- **Incumbent renewed within the last 90 days**
  `set: 2026-03-10` · `evidence: cycle extends past our fiscal year` · `confidence: moderate`
- **Champion is below director level with no executive access**
  `set: 2026-02-14` · `evidence: 5 of 7 stalled at business case` · `confidence: moderate`

## Anti-disqualifiers

Things that look bad and are not. Write these down too — they stop an agent
filtering out accounts your instinct would keep.

- **Small current team size** — often means the problem is unsolved, not absent
- **No existing tooling** — slower, but not disqualifying on its own
