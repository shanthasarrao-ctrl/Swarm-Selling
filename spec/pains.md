# Pains

> **Replace everything below.** Sample content from a fictional vendor.

The pains you actually remove, in the buyer's language rather than yours.

The second line of each entry is the most useful thing in this file: the words
the buyer uses. Agents should search for that language in filings, job postings
and reviews, not for your product category.

---

## 1. Volume growing faster than headcount

- **Their words**: "we can't hire our way out of this", "the queue keeps growing"
- **Who feels it**: VP Operations, Director of Support
- **Evidence it exists**: headcount requisitions outpacing revenue growth;
  overtime mentioned in earnings calls; hiring for the same role repeatedly
- **What we remove**: the linear relationship between volume and staffing
- `set: 2026-01-15` · `confidence: strong`

## 2. Inconsistent quality across the team

- **Their words**: "it depends who picks it up", "we can't guarantee an outcome"
- **Who feels it**: Head of Quality, COO
- **Evidence it exists**: variance metrics in public reporting; review sites
  mentioning inconsistency
- **What we remove**: variance, not volume
- `set: 2026-02-03` · `confidence: moderate`

## 3. No visibility into why outcomes differ

- **Their words**: "we find out too late", "we're flying blind"
- **Who feels it**: RevOps, Analytics
- **Evidence it exists**: recent analytics hires; BI tooling in the stack
- **What we remove**: the lag between something going wrong and knowing
- `set: 2026-02-03` · `confidence: weak`
