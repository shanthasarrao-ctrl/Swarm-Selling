## L-2026-03 · Retail · ~$180k · Lost to Incumbent A

- **Stated reason**: price
- **Real reason**: no executive sponsor; champion left in month four
- **Earliest detectable signal**: champion had no budget authority at first call
- **What would have changed it**: disqualify at week two, or force exec access
- **Pattern candidate**: champion seniority → stall at business case

## L-2026-05 · Financial services · ~$240k · Lost to status quo

- **Stated reason**: timing, revisit next year
- **Real reason**: we sold to the problem owner, not the budget owner
- **Earliest detectable signal**: budget holder never joined a call
- **What would have changed it**: multithread by week three
- **Pattern candidate**: single-threaded deals stall

## L-2026-07 · Travel · ~$95k · Lost to Challenger B

- **What would have changed it**: not pursuing it
- **Pattern candidate**: sub-floor accounts lose on price
