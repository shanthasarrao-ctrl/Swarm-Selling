# Voice

> Replace with your own. This is a sample.

Read by `agents/critique/message-rewrite.md`.

Without this file, three passes of critique and rewrite converge on competent,
generic B2B prose. The system optimises your voice out of your own emails and
the result reads like every other automated message the buyer deleted that
morning.

## How I write

- Short sentences. One idea each.
- No more than 90 words in a cold email. No more than 4 lines in a LinkedIn note.
- Lead with the observation about them. Never with who I am or where I work.
- One ask, and it is small. A question, not a meeting request, on first contact.
- Plain words. Not "leverage", "synergies", "solutions", "reach out", "circle
  back", "touch base", "at scale", "unlock", "empower".
- No exclamation marks.
- No flattery openers. Not "loved your post", not "impressive quarter".
- No fake personalisation. If the only thing I know is their job title, the
  message should not pretend otherwise — it should not be sent.

## What I never do

- Claim a result I cannot cite
- Mention a competitor by name in a first touch
- Use a template that would read identically with the company name swapped
- Send anything I would be embarrassed to have forwarded to their boss

## The test

If the message would still make sense with a different company's name in it,
it isn't finished.
