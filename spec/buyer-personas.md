Definitions live here rather than being improvised per run, so that when a
message improves you know the message changed and not the judge.

## VP Operations

- **What their week looks like**: back-to-back, 60+ emails a day, at least four
  of them cold and pitching something adjacent to yours
- **What they care about**: headcount, queue volume, whether the number moves
  this quarter
- **What they ignore**: anything about your product before anything about their
  problem; any email over 120 words
- **How they'd describe their problem**: "we can't hire our way out of this"
- **What makes them reply**: a specific, checkable observation about their
  operation that they did not expect an outsider to know

## Director of Support

- **What their week looks like**: firefighting, weekly metrics review
- **What they care about**: quality variance, agent churn, escalations
- **What they ignore**: strategic framing, transformation language
- **How they'd describe their problem**: "it depends who picks it up"
- **What makes them reply**: evidence you understand the operational detail
- `set: 2026-02-01` · `confidence: weak`

## CFO

- **What their week looks like**: board prep, three functions asking for budget
- **What they care about**: what it replaces, payback period, downside risk
- **What they ignore**: feature claims, anything without a number
- **What makes them reply**: usually nothing. They are not your entry point.
- `set: 2026-02-01` · `confidence: weak`
