## Firmographic

- **Employee count**: 500–5,000
  `set: 2026-01-15` · `evidence: 14 of last 18 closed-won fell in this band`
- **Geography**: North America, UK
  `set: 2026-01-15` · `evidence: no support coverage outside these regions`
- **Industry**: retail, financial services, travel
  `set: 2026-03-02` · `evidence: three verticals where the compliance story lands`

## Structural

- **Has a dedicated team owning the problem we solve**
  `set: 2026-01-15` · `evidence: deals without a named owner stalled at 3x the rate`
- **Existing tooling in the category** (replacement, not first purchase)
  `set: 2026-02-20` · `evidence: greenfield deals took 2x longer and closed at half the rate`

## Trigger conditions

Not required, but they move an account up the list.

- New executive in the function we sell into, 0–9 months in seat
- Public commitment to a programme our product serves
- Hiring for roles that imply the problem exists

## Confidence

Each criterion above should be one of:

- **Strong** — 10+ supporting deals
- **Moderate** — 4–9 deals
- **Weak** — fewer than 4, or inherited from someone else

Weak criteria are candidates, not rules. Agents should treat them as tie-breakers
rather than filters.
